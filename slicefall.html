A reflex-puzzle game about stacking narrowing blocks until one falls down.

STACK. SLICE. FALL.

Try to stack four block pieces moving back and forth. Any part of the block not perfectly stacked is sliced off. Completely misplace a block and it's game over.

LONGER COMBO, HIGHER SCORE, MORE REWARD

Fill an entire row with blocks to make it explode for points and a combo multiplier. Perfectly place blocks to keep the combo going until you can detonate another row.

CHANGE UP HOW THINGS LOOK AND PLAY

Utilize power ups to make things easier and blow up as many rows as possible. Mix up the colors using a variety of palettes or make your own instead!

THREE GAMEMODES TO MASTER

Endless - explode as many rows as possible until it's too fast.

Puzzle - navigate through a randomly generated tower of blocks to explode a set amount of rows.

Tower - explode a row as high as possible, leaving unbreakable barriers in your wake.

Can you stack on sliver to keep it all from falling apart?
